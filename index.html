Ndalem · The house

Teak, courtyards and the colour of sogan.

Sogan is the deep brown of classic Yogyakarta batik, dyed from the bark of the soga tree. Our house is built around it: carved teak pillars, an open pendhapa for gamelan evenings, and courtyards that stay cool in the afternoon.

Location

In the old heart of Yogyakarta

We are inside the old city, near the Kraton and Taman Sari. Prambanan and Borobudur are an easy drive for sunrise, and we meet guests at Yogyakarta International Airport (YIA) or Tugu station.
